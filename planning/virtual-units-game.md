# Virtual units game — D-18 task list

Status: confirmed product rules; game implementation is in progress and is not deployed. This is a separate play-money game under Picks, not a real sportsbook wager, the record-only Betting Challenge, a private actual-bet journal, Guess the Spread/Total, or Best Bets. One unit represents $100 virtual. Settlement rules are confirmed as D-26 (October 9, 2026); see the [settlement runbook](#settlement-runbook--d-26) below.

Owner: Sean Balsiger · Version 4 · October 9, 2026.

## Confirmed play rules

- A player can place one spread pick and one total pick on the same NFL matchup, but cannot place a second pick in the same market and matchup. First placement and edits are allowed until that game's kickoff. Keep every prior side, line, odds, unit amount and timestamp in an audit.
- Make entry button-driven: choose a spread side or Over/Under, then 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3 or another permitted unit amount, then confirm. There is no starting bankroll or stake cap; a pick is not rejected for an insufficient virtual balance.
- For each side, offer the most favorable **point line** from the latest retained named-book quotes. If points tie, choose the better American odds. More points favor the selected spread team; a lower total favors Over, and a higher total favors Under. Show the actual book, line, odds and quote observation/collection times. Show consensus separately as comparison data, never as a book offer. No manual line or odds entry is required for this flow.
- New saved quotes update the offered buttons. Already placed picks retain their saved line and odds unless the player explicitly edits before kickoff. **Do not impose a quote-age cutoff on this virtual game:** the latest retained named-book quote for that matchup and market remains selectable before kickoff when collection fails or a successful refresh omits that book's market. A newer saved quote from another book still competes for the best offered point line. Label the saved quote's source, observation/collection time and age prominently, save its exact historical terms and never call an aged quote a current executable offer. If no named-book quote has ever been saved for a matchup's spread or total, disable only that market's pick button until a genuine quote is saved. Keep other games and markets open; never fabricate a line or silently substitute consensus.
- Weekly standings qualification requires **at least three picks OR at least 3 units**. A week below both thresholds is omitted from weekly standings, while its settled picks still count in that player's personal overall record and ROI. An unreplaced voided pick retains its original pick and unit qualification credit. If the same market and matchup is replaced within the same competition week, count one qualifying pick at the replacement's unit stake, even if lower than the original; do not count both placements. If the rescheduled game moves to a different competition week, leave the voided original's pick/unit qualification credit in its original week and count a fresh pick only in the new week at its own unit stake.
- A postponement staying on the same `America/Los_Angeles` calendar date carries the saved pick, line, odds and units to the revised kickoff. A move to a different Pacific calendar date voids the original, returns its virtual stake, retains the audit and permits a fresh pick at the rescheduled market. Store kickoff timestamps in UTC for evidence and show the Pacific date to players. During an automated schedule-feed outage, the commissioner may enter a revised kickoff only from a verifiable official league notice; save the notice link and time, actor, old/new kickoff and change history. This changes the pick deadline, not result grading. A possible cancellation or no contest leaves picks pending while status is uncertain; verified cancellation or no contest from a permitted official source voids them, returns virtual stakes and preserves original pick/unit qualification credit. Notify affected players of these voids with a persistent in-app notice and opt-in push and email once those external paths are connected and verified. An unconfirmed cancellation report does not settle or void picks.
- Settle only from a permitted, verified automatic final-score feed. Commissioner-entered finals do not settle this game. A win earns net units from saved American odds (1 unit at −110 earns about +0.9091); a loss costs the staked units; a push returns the stake for zero net. ROI is net units divided by units risked on settled picks; pending and void picks are excluded. Track this game's own W-L-P, pending/void counts, units risked, net units and ROI by week, season and market. Whenever that automatic feed verifies a corrected final, even after weekly standings appear, regrade affected picks from their original saved terms, append a before/after audit, recalculate records, net units, ROI and standings, and notify affected players. No verified automatic feed is connected for this game yet, so picks would remain pending.

## Settlement — D-26

Confirmed October 9, 2026 (see [D-26](../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-26--virtual-units-settlement-october-9-2026) and the [rules row](../docs/RULES.md)). A pick settles against the **final score** with the line and odds saved at placement:

| Outcome | Rule | Units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Win | Spread: the chosen side covers the saved line. Total: the final combined score is over/under the saved total as chosen. | + units × (decimal payout − 1); decimal payout = 1 + (odds > 0 ? odds / 100 : 100 / \|odds\|), the same conversion as `shared/social-bets.mjs` `socialPotentialProfit()`; the product rounded once to four places from the unrounded multiplier (1U at −110 → +0.9091U, 0.5U at −110 → +0.4545U, 2U at +120 → +2.4U); `payoutDecimal` on the result is a rounded display value that never feeds the arithmetic |
| Loss | The chosen side does not cover / the total lands on the other side. | − staked units |
| Push | The final lands exactly on the saved line. | 0; stake returned |
| Void | The game is postponed, cancelled or otherwise unplayed. | 0; stake returned; excluded from record and ROI; D-18 qualification credit kept |

Settlement never changes a pick's saved side, line, odds, units or timestamps (the `selection` JSON, `created_at` and `updated_at` are untouched). It writes `status` and `result` (`outcome`, `status`, `reason`, `home`, `away`, `margin`, `netUnits`, `returnedUnits`, `payoutDecimal`, `version`, `settledAt`, `observedAt`, `source`, `sourceUrl`, `previous`) and appends a `settled`, `resettled` or `voided` event with `before`/`after` to the pick's `audit`. Each game's finals are an append-only ledger (`gtl_virtual_units_settlements`, one row per version); re-running identical finals writes no ledger row and leaves every settled pick as it is, grading only picks placed or re-saved since (the save path resets a row to pending) so nothing stays pending behind a recorded result; a different final appends the next version and re-grades from the original saved terms. A `postponed` void is terminal for that placement (D-18: a fresh pick at the rescheduled market replaces it through the ordinary save path); a `cancelled` void is a ruling and is re-graded if a later verified final corrects it. The reader (`gtl_read_virtual_units`) now returns `results` (latest settlement per slate game) and `settlement` (`settledGames`, `settledPicks`, `pendingPicks`, `lastSettledAt`); record, net units and ROI count settled picks only, and `qualificationPicks`/`qualificationUnits` still count every placed pick.

### Settlement runbook — D-26

Step 1 was run from the cloud session on October 9 at 18:46 UTC (hosted version `20261009184601`); steps 2–4 need the `gtl-api` deploy and a published week with finals.

1. **Migration, in repo order (done: hosted `20261009184601`).** Apply `supabase/migrations/20261009170000_gtl_virtual_units_settlement.sql` through the Supabase MCP `apply_migration` (or the SQL editor), the same way the five October 9 files were applied; do not run `npx supabase db push` as-is (see the [release checklist](../docs/SOCIAL_COMMUNITY.md#release-checklist--october-9-2026)). Every earlier repo file must already be on the hosted project; the only new dependency is `20261008240000_gtl_virtual_units.sql`. Record the hosted version in [RELEASE_2026_10_09.md](../docs/RELEASE_2026_10_09.md).
2. **API.** `npx supabase functions deploy gtl-api` from `main` ships the route and the reader fields; the frontend deploy (`npm run build:cloud` → Pages) ships the settled-state screen. Until the API deploy the hosted database carries the function but nothing calls it.
3. **Settle a week** once its finals are published in nflverse (usually Tuesday morning Pacific after the Monday night game; the file updates within hours of a final). Either Virtual Units → Settle finals as the commissioner, or:
   ```sh
   node scripts/virtual-units-settle.mjs --season 2026 --week 6              # dry run: prints the finals payload
   SUPABASE_URL=https://<ref>.supabase.co SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY=<from the dashboard, never committed> \
   node scripts/virtual-units-settle.mjs --season 2026 --week 6 --apply      # settles; prints settled/corrected/unchanged
   ```
   Re-running is safe: identical finals end `unchanged`. A changed final ends `corrected` and re-grades. A game still without a final is listed under `pending` and its picks stay pending.
4. **Verify.** `select game_id,version,status,home,away,picks_settled,settled_at from public.gtl_virtual_units_settlements where season=2026 and week=6 order by game_id,version;` and the Virtual Units screen's record/net/ROI for one player.
5. **Rollback.** There is no destructive path. A wrong final is corrected by settling again with the right score (new version); nothing is deleted. Do not drop the ledger.
