# Guess The Line — owner visual review

Owner: Sean Balsiger · Version 1 · November 4, 2026

Open [the board](index.html) in a browser. It contains 39 screen pairs (78 PNGs)
in Dark and Light, nine review areas, reminder-state illustrations, eight
functional check sequences and X inspiration links. Every new sign-off needs a
visual first. Existing explicit selections remain valid.

Theme buttons swap both the board and screenshot variants. Captures scroll
inside their frames and have full-image links. Review notes are stored in this
browser when local storage is available. Export downloads JSON. These are draft
notes only; no approval, account change or product write is submitted.

## Provenance

The [manifest](manifest.json) records every source family, original filename,
image dimensions and SHA-256. Images were copied unchanged.

- Home: two 390px screens in both themes from D-69 step 2.
- Play: thirteen fixture screens in both themes from D-69 step 1.
- Research: seven fixture screens in both themes from D-69 step 3. These
  precede its final CI font/wrapping fixes.
- Bets, Fantasy, Account, EPL and reminders: existing versioned design/qa or
  design-study directories, named in the manifest.
- Functional sequences and permission-state illustrations: explanatory HTML
  using current GTL tokens. They are not captured live results or OS dialogs.

The first three sets came from the fixture screenshot folders documented in the
[Play plan](../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-03-design-overhaul-step1-play.md),
[Home plan](../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-design-overhaul-step2-home.md) and
[Research plan](../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-design-overhaul-step3-research.md).
Only their fixture PNGs were copied, not temporary test scripts or live account
data. Fixed navigation can appear partway through a tall full-page capture;
use the known capture context rather than inferring a phone overlap defect.

The board preserves existing app-brand choices from docs/BRAND_GUIDE.md and
design/tokens-next.json. It loads the approved Google Fonts stylesheet with
system fallbacks. Images remain available without a font/network connection.

## Limits

The November 3 Fantasy captures do not show the newer report-card layout.
The linked EPL four-screen concept is historical and contains an obsolete NBA
option. Current Fantasy report, EPL confirmation/claim, loading and motion
states still require specific visuals/evidence before those sign-offs.

No private player records or credentials are included. Sample names, lines,
dates and statistics are fictional fixtures; do not use them as live data.
The linked X posts are third-party references; their media were not copied.

See the [acceptance record](../../docs/OWNER_ACCEPTANCE_2026_11_04.md) for the

technical checks. A visual choice does not prove data correctness, privacy,
notification delivery, migration application or deployment.
