09 / Functional checks

These visual sequences make each check reviewable. They do not mark a backend feature accepted. Current-screen captures and real evidence must accompany any later sign-off where marked missing.

Saved data

  1. View an existing saved item
  2. Reload or reopen the app
  3. Same item and receipt remain visible

A capture illustrates the state; readback verifies persistence.

Reveal & privacy

  1. Before the game's permitted reveal
  2. Opponent guess stays hidden
  3. After the allowed gate, available guess appears

Use fixture accounts; preserve D-31 and private rosters.

Pick a Team

  1. Saved ranked backup becomes available on your turn
  2. Assign the highest available team, or wait if none
  3. Show the recorded-team confirmation

A current confirmation capture and hosted readback are still needed.

Scoring & references

  1. Lock in with a qualifying fresh reference
  2. Keep the saved reference and its time
  3. Show result without replacing that reference

Collector observations and scoring evidence remain technical work.

Notifications

  1. Explicitly enable on the intended device
  2. Receive a qualifying notification
  3. Tap reaches the correct app screen

Mockups do not verify operating-system delivery.

One-time moments

  1. Fresh result appears as NEW
  2. Open the result: permitted moment plays
  3. Return later: no replay; reduced motion stays quiet

Still images cannot establish motion or one-time behavior.

Updates & icons

  1. Existing Home Screen installation
  2. Load the released version without losing saved data
  3. Correct icon, theme and routes persist

Owner navigation direction does not verify cache or icon recovery.

Retirement & expansion

  1. Show exact proposed removal or future scope
  2. Review preservation and rollback evidence
  3. Obtain specific approval for that action

No deletion, migration apply or future-feature approval follows from this board.
