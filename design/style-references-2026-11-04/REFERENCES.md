# Guess The Line — 87 public design references

Owner: Sean Balsiger · Version 1 · November 4, 2026

Sean requested 50–100 varied references before selecting 5–10 genuinely different styles for whole-app mockups. Glass is one option, not the theme of every option. No new mockups are included in this collection.

Open [the visual library](index.html). Filter by search group, mark favorites and export their IDs. There are 84 publisher-provided preview links and 3 source-only references. Source text/metadata were retrieved; the collection is for owner visual selection, not a claim that every preview was independently visually audited. Images remain remotely hosted by their publishers. Availability may change. Concepts and isolated components are included for breadth; they do not establish a production-ready whole-app system. Private Twitter bookmarks were not accessed. Mobbin was unavailable because its connector requires a paid plan; no upgrade was made.
