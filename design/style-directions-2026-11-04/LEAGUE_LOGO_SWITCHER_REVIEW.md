1. **Rounded glass squares — recommended.** Clear selection, full-size marks,
   familiar shape and the closest fit to the selected cards.
2. **Glass circles.** Softer and more playful, with equal league weight.
3. **Compact glass capsules.** Lower profile with an inset lime selection edge.
4. **Lifted lime glass.** More prominent active tint and a small upward offset.
5. **Quiet glass + underline.** Neutral faces and a separate lime selection bar.

Each league has its own glass tile, border and shadow. The shared layout has
no background or shadow. NFL/EPL text is replaced in the switcher; accessible
full names, title hints, native button semantics and keyboard focus remain.
Original league colors/proportions stay intact. The official white Premier
League lion is used on dark; the official purple variant is used on light.
The G, existing initials, selected themes and type stay as selected.

This is an isolated comparison, not a change to previously approved screens.
The context cards are fictional visual illustrations with static actions:
they do not assert fixtures, current prices, draft turns or available account
data. No actual app routes, reads, claims, saves or integrations are connected.

## Sources and verification

[source register](visual-assets/league-marks/sources.json) preserves exact URLs,

[NFL Shield & Logo System](https://designplaybook.nfl.com/about) and

used for identification only. No affiliation, endorsement or production
reuse license is claimed.
