## Review scope

43 core states, a revised manual-entry placement, five scrolled sheet pairs and four explanation/rule pairs: **106 phone images**. Loading, failed, empty, hidden, wrong-account/week, expired, uncertain, busy and fixed confirmed states are deliberately separate. A confirmed or shared-looking state never follows a preview action.

The league switcher/invitations, reaction chooser, Menu destinations and general offline/startup/page/tab recovery already have scoped approvals. Native header/tab placement and the layer-open-failure toast are reviewed here. The navigation body is an illustrative context card; it does not replace the previously reviewed destination content. The preview tab slot map is empty, so notification counts are not invented.

New avatar/photo options, new award/celebration exploration and parked Signal assets remain held. Hidden NBA navigation and retained links are preserved; retained-route visuals remain a separate coverage item. Whole-app implementation and production acceptance are unfinished.
