## Operating limits and remaining work

Week 5's configured prediction deadline has passed. Closed controls were verified;
no real test guesses or deadline changes were made. Historical guesses without a
qualifying submission-time reference remain **Unscored**. Newer quotes are never
used to manufacture missing historical references. A previously saved snapshot
can remain visible with its own stale label.

The twelve-hour collector and existing free limits were not changed: the shared
fifteen-minute cooldown and four-attempt daily cap remain. The proposed five-minute
cooldown/seven-attempt alternative is not approved or enabled. No odds-provider
request was made for deployment verification, and no scheduled boundary was
observed during this release.

Outside forecast feeds, public betting activity, Action Network/Caesars,
phone push/email/SMS delivery, physical-phone acceptance and hosted simultaneous
save verification remain incomplete. Model availability and bet records must not
be mistaken for a validated prediction service or guaranteed betting outcomes.
Full acceptance and open product work remain in the project register.

The security advisor retains two pre-existing warnings:
[pg_net in the public schema](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/database-linter?lint=0014_extension_in_public)
and [leaked-password protection disabled](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security#password-strength-and-leaked-password-protection).
The 32 no-policy informational findings describe intentional server-only tables.
No new security warning, broader browser access or paid service was introduced.
