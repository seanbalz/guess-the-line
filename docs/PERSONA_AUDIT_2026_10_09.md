# Persona audit — October 9, 2026

Owner: Sean Balsiger · Version 2 · Updated 2026-10-09 · Scope: the D-21/D-22 Community home, Picks tab (ChipPicker), groups screen, the D-23 sit-out flow, the chip-entry group ballot and the error paths around them. Version 1 ran against `main` at `d0a4e9f`; version 2 re-ran everything after merging `main` at `2a76396` (D-23 data rule and sit-out, D-24 Monday 12:00 AM PT week open with the "Preview / publish early (fallback)" panel, PR #19 chip entry on group screens, PR #21 clock pin, PR #22 Edge board phone layout and TeamMatchup label fix). Rows note where main changed the result.

Seven simulated users worked through the real app (built with `--mode cloud-test`) against the real Edge handler and the real migrations on a local PGlite database, with only Supabase Auth mocked. Nothing here touched hosted data. The harness is reusable: `npm run test:personas` re-runs every persona, re-takes every screenshot and rewrites the findings file. The specs assert only what is true today, so the suite is green and the findings are the output, not the pass/fail.

**Update (PR #34, 2026-10-09):** quick wins 2–11 below shipped on `main`; the rows they close are marked in the findings table and the persona specs now assert the fixed behaviour (the counts in "Results in one paragraph" describe version 2, before that PR). Bigger changes are unchanged. The review of PR #34 (same day) tightened the quick wins without reopening any: every busy save-bar control (Market, "Sit out this week", Save draft, Submit and the banner's buttons) and the post controls are `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled`, so the pressed button keeps focus while it reads "Saving…" and a banner's Retry or "Got it" hands focus on instead of dropping it on `<body>`; under forced colours a selected chip no longer takes a Highlight fill (Chromium backplates HighlightText on Highlight, which left the chosen label a white block) and shows its state with a 3px Highlight border, a Highlight check icon and its label in the forced button colours, which the low-vision spec now verifies from the painted pixels; the Submit hint is an always-mounted live region that describes the button; the chip helper is dismissed per market; the skip link reads "Skip to picks due" when that is where it lands; "Failed to fetch" reads as "No connection…" on the feed and inside posts too; a D-17 submission 409 keeps Retry (only a bare 409 is a revision conflict); the 401 banner puts Retry first and says that "Sign in again" clears the screen; a submitted ballot with an incomplete edit is explained instead of posted; the closed week says whether a ballot was submitted; and the team chip's check sits outside the name's text flow.

## Method

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| Harness (PGlite seed, handler, auth mock, finding/axe/screenshot/target helpers) | `tests/cloud-browser/personas/harness.mjs` |
| One spec per persona | `tests/cloud-browser/personas/{first-timer,phone,power,low-vision,motor,impatient,confused}.spec.js` |
| Static checks: token contrast, declared sizes under 44px, reduced-motion and forced-colors coverage | `scripts/persona-static-checks.mjs` → `.local/personas/static.json` |
| Structured findings (persona, screen, severity 1–4, WCAG ref, experience, fix, screenshot, evidence) | `.local/personas/findings.json` |
| axe-core WCAG 2.1 A/AA scan log, one row per screen | `.local/personas/axe-scans.json` |
| Screenshots | `.local/previews/personas/<persona>-<screen>.png` |

Severity: 1 blocks the task or corrupts data · 2 major friction, likely abandonment or a wrong pick · 3 moderate confusion or an accessibility gap with a workaround · 4 polish.

Each persona test seeds a fresh database: Week 5 slate (2–14 games), three profiles (Ben, Troy, Luke), a three-book market snapshot so D-17 submission passes, and a deadline of 11:59 PM PT today (so the week is open under D-24) unless the scenario needs a closed or not-yet-open week. Auth is `page.route` returning a fixed session; every `gtl-api` request goes through `createCloudHandler` with PGlite behind `rpc`, `storage.membership` and `weekly`. The fixture answers the D-17 preflight as "eligible cached references" unless a scenario sets `net.quotesUnavailable`. Network latency, offline, injected status codes and session expiry are switches on `persona.net`.

## Results in one paragraph

79 findings on the merged tree: 0 severity-1, 25 severity-2, 35 severity-3, 19 severity-4 (several are the same issue seen by more than one persona; the table below is de-duplicated to 44 rows). The one severity-1 bug from version 1 (stale "0" chips in the Total ballot) was fixed on `main` by PR #19 before this merge; the spec now asserts the fix as a regression guard. One new severity-2 regression arrived with PR #19 and was still present after PR #22's TeamMatchup fix (at 1024px the group ballot's matchup column collapsed to 30px and full team names wrapped letter by letter, row 2); **it was fixed on `main` by PR #26** after this version ran. axe-core reported **zero WCAG 2.1 A/AA violations on all 24 scanned screens** (dark and light, including the sit-out dialog and the D-24 not-open states), and the token contrast check passes AA for every text/background pair the Community and chip stylesheets use. The problems are not in the automated-checkable layer. They are in explanation (nobody tells a newcomer what −3 or "total" means on the Community tab; the group ballot now has that sentence), in state handling (unsaved picks vanish on a tab switch, reload or back press; errors are raw strings with no action), and in keyboard cost (382 presses for a 14-game ballot).
